# The Monzù House — Brand OS v0.1

Status: usable draft

Effective review date: 2026-09-04

## Operating summary

The House is the central identity. Restaurant, Piazza and Cellar are distinct expressions under it, not interchangeable venues. The existing neoclassical house and known address in Kifissia are part of the identity and must not be presented as a new destination.

The Restaurant is the celebrated-dinner expression: Italian-inspired, dinner-only, precise and calm. It is not fine dining and must never be described as a «γαστρονομική εμπειρία». Current management-confirmed hours are Tuesday–Sunday, 19:00–02:00; the kitchen closes at 23:30.

Piazza is the relaxed-gathering expression: wine, genuinely shareable small plates and a lower-intervention service rhythm. It must not become a smaller Restaurant. Its opening status, days and hours remain unknown and must not be published without confirmation.

Cellar is the slow-discovery expression: curated wine and spirits, relevant guidance, take-home purchase, repeat orders and gifting. It is not a large-range retailer and must never be called «κάβα». Its opening status, days, hours and complete live offer remain unknown.

## How Monzù behaves

Quality should be demonstrated through conduct, food, detail and relevance rather than announced. Decisions should protect the House, preserve harmony, put people at ease, favour thoughtful choices over novelty and remove what is unnecessary. Price is not a proxy for belonging or quality.

Communication must be precise and credible. Spoken Greek should be natural, with unnecessary English avoided. Internal references such as “quiet luxury,” “old money,” “aristocracy,” “premium” and “exclusive club” are not public positioning claims.

Current founder approval remains required for brand, creative, menus, operations, partnerships and customer communication. No discounts, price-led promotions or influencer collaborations are permitted under the current management rule.

## Current commercial focus

Current priorities are time-sensitive and live in `operating-context/`: give Piazza a strong market head start, encourage visits and frequency, protect Cellar continuity through the Cava Vinifera transition, and reduce the Restaurant's decline without lowering standards.

## Reliability boundaries

- No approved canonical manifesto or long-form public copy is stored in v0.1.
- Do not invent products, dishes, prices, events, audiences, competitors, hours or targets.
- Do not use planned launch dates as proof that an experience is open.
- Do not treat approved creative as a universal rule or rejected work as a universal prohibition.
- Check `HUMAN_REVIEW_REQUIRED.md` when a task depends on unresolved operating, menu, language, visual or measurement information.
