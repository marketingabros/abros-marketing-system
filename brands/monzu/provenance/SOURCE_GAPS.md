# Monzù Brand OS material source gaps

Audit date: 2026-09-03

Only gaps that could materially change marketing execution are listed. An empty template folder is not a gap when a master source covers the subject, and an empty connector response is not treated as proof that a Drive folder is empty.

Priority meaning: `P0` blocks reliable ingestion or creates immediate execution risk; `P1` can be declared and handled during ingestion; `P2` can wait until the relevant work begins.

## BRAND

- **P0 — Founder-approved durable brand source:** Brand Knowledge v0.1 and v0.2 explicitly remain working documents. Extract which statements are founder-confirmed, approve a durable version, and keep consolidated wording labelled as working until then.
- **P1 — House naming and public descriptor:** Confirm the canonical public relationship and naming syntax for “Monzù”, “The Monzù House”, and each experience across Greek and English. The architecture is clear; exact public naming mechanics are not fully approved.
- **P1 — Values versus strategic pillars:** The founder-selected values (Honest, Craftsmanship, Consistency) and later working pillars (Discernment, Craft, Stewardship) are explicitly not interchangeable. Approve whether the latter remain internal decision aids or become Brand OS rules.

## BUSINESS

- **P0 — Verified baselines and definitions:** Validate the reported Restaurant decline, Cellar rolling sales, available Piazza capacity, covers, revenue, margin, average spend, repeat rate, and the periods/data systems behind them before they drive optimisation.
- **P1 — Competitive frame:** No approved direct competitor set or comparison dimensions are established. This limits PPC conquest analysis, positioning checks and market interpretation; do not invent competitors.
- **P1 — Offer status and economics:** Confirm which Cellar and Piazza products/services are actually live, their price architecture, fulfilment/delivery boundaries and commercial constraints.

## EXPERIENCES

- **P0 — Piazza current offer:** Confirm the served/final menu, opening status, operating hours/days, reservation versus walk-in model, capacity, and which intended dayparts are actually live.
- **P0 — Cellar current offer:** Confirm opening status, hours/days, current inventory/service categories, gifting and delivery scope, and whether food provisions and cigars are live, planned or rejected.
- **P1 — Experience boundary tests:** Convert approved Restaurant-versus-Piazza food and service distinctions into concrete review tests using current examples. Do not turn working examples into permanent rules without approval.

## AUDIENCES

- **P1 — Priority audience decisions:** Validated segments, priority order, geography, visit frequency, decision journeys, objections and tourism relevance are not established. Existing guest situations and management observations are useful hypotheses, not personas.
- **P1 — First-party evidence:** A compliant source for reservation, CRM, Cellar-customer and repeat-visit analysis is not identified. Define ownership, consent/access and minimum useful fields before audience claims are made.

## OBJECTIVES

- **P0 — Target ownership and measurement:** Confirm owners, baselines, deadlines, source systems and reporting cadence for the 90-day Restaurant, Piazza and Cellar objectives. Management targets are high authority, but not yet measurement-ready.
- **P0 — Current launch-state reset:** The rollout plan's June–September phases are dated planning evidence. Confirm what actually launched, when, and which objectives remain active as of ingestion.
- **P1 — Twelve-month outcomes and budgets:** The top outcomes, budgets, dependencies and trade-off order are not confirmed.
- **P1 — Channel targets:** Qualified lead, acquisition cost, reservation conversion, visit, retention and CRM engagement definitions/targets are missing.

## TONE OF VOICE

- **P0 — Rule/example separation:** Produce an approved statement-level list separating permanent wording rules from examples, AI expansions and campaign alternatives in the messaging guide.
- **P0 — Resolve vocabulary conflicts:** Decide the permitted contexts, if any, for discovery language; remove or explicitly archive examples using `κάβα` for Cellar or `γαστρονομική εμπειρία`/fine-dining positioning for Restaurant.
- **P1 — Language system:** Approve Greek/English naming, transliteration, accent use (`Monzù` versus `Monzu` in technical contexts), spoken-versus-written conventions, and experience-specific phone/sign-off language.

## VISUAL IDENTITY

- **P0 — Canonical colour tokens:** Identify the exact current palette file and approve machine-readable values. Numbered SCSS/PDF/SVG variants exist without explicit per-file precedence.
- **P0 — Production typography:** Confirm licensed font files, weights, Greek support, web/print usage rights and approved fallbacks for the fonts shown on the final identity board.
- **P1 — Export manifest:** Map each approved House/Restaurant/Piazza/Cellar lockup and icon to intended use, format, colourway, minimum size and clear-space rule; label derivatives versus masters.
- **P1 — Historical boundary:** Confirm which pre-final colour, logo and icon files are superseded so tooling cannot select them by recency or filename similarity.

## ASSETS

- **P0 — Photography direction:** Management explicitly declares this a gap. Define approved subject matter, composition, lighting, people/privacy, retouching, crops and experience-specific differences.
- **P0 — Approved photography manifest:** Resolve the root guide's “2026 Photoshoot” reference against the connector-visible Photography structure, then list the approved selects and their Drive IDs.
- **P0 — Rights and consent:** Record photographer/licence, usage territories/channels/duration, talent and location releases, and expiry restrictions for selected images/video/illustrations.
- **P1 — Asset status metadata:** Label final, approved, working, rejected and historical files without copying originals into Git.

## MARKETING

- **P0 — Google Ads data contamination:** All three Google report exports include unrelated Homebrew rows. Establish account/customer identifiers and a repeatable exclusion rule before analysis.
- **P0 — Performance metric definitions:** Confirm currency, timezone, attribution windows, conversion actions, duplicate/secondary conversions, spend tax treatment and reporting period for Meta and Google exports.
- **P1 — Social indexing and completeness:** Re-run or manually verify Approved, Rejected and Performance inventories after Drive indexing settles. Record counts and IDs; do not assume connector-empty means absent.
- **P1 — Website content baseline:** Capture current URLs, page ownership, last-approved copy, analytics/search-console scope and update workflow. Screenshots are point-in-time evidence only.
- **P1 — Campaign and CRM history:** Identify any current plan, email/CRM activity and results needed for retention work. Their incompleteness does not block Brand OS v0.1.

## OPERATIONS

- **P0 — Piazza and Cellar operating facts:** Exact opening dates/status, operating days/hours, reservation/walk-in rules and service constraints remain unconfirmed.
- **P0 — Restaurant fact distribution:** Management has confirmed Tuesday–Sunday, guest hours 19:00–02:00 and kitchen close 23:30. Identify every customer-facing system that still carries 19:00–01:00 or an unknown kitchen close and correct it through normal human-approved operations.
- **P1 — Restaurant booking and capacity rules:** Confirm reservation channels, walk-in policy, seating/capacity, last booking, cancellations, private events and holiday exceptions before campaigns make availability claims.
- **P1 — Source ownership:** Name the management owner and review frequency for operating facts so temporary changes do not become permanent brand rules or stale campaign claims.
