## Classification rules

- `AUTHORITATIVE`: explicitly documented as current/final or explicitly confirmed by management. Authority applies only to the source's approved statements, not automatically to every sentence in a mixed document.
- `OBSERVED`: evidence of actual output, behaviour, performance, or a management observation that is not a permanent brand rule.
- `HISTORICAL`: superseded or time-bound evidence retained for context and learning.
- `UNKNOWN`: accessible evidence does not establish finality, approval, currentness, or the fact in question.
- `CONFLICTING`: reliable sources disagree. The statement stays conflicting unless a higher-authority source explicitly resolves it.

Authority levels below follow the Phase 2A hierarchy, from `1` (current explicit management decision) to `10` (AI draft/brainstorm). “Approved” means approval was explicit in the evidence; a filename, recency, or folder location alone does not prove approval.
