# Monzù near-term human review required

These are the smallest management decisions that would materially improve or unblock near-term marketing execution.

## 1. Piazza and Cellar live operating status

- **Question:** Are Piazza and Cellar currently open? If so, what are their opening dates, days, guest hours, booking/walk-in rules and service limitations?
- **Why it matters:** Planned September timing is not evidence of launch.
- **Affects:** Social/PPC publication, website, listings, launch messaging and customer responses.
- **Current evidence:** MGMT-001 records intended early-September openings but explicitly leaves exact dates and hours unknown.
- **Options:** Confirm live with exact facts; confirm not yet live with approved “coming soon” status; or provide a dated interim status.

## 2. Current served menus and offers

- **Question:** Which Restaurant and Piazza menu files are final and currently served, and which Cellar products/services are live?
- **Why it matters:** Multiple candidate files exist and seasonal/product facts expire.
- **Affects:** Menu workflow, social/PPC offer claims, website and customer communication.
- **Current evidence:** MENU-001/003/005 and EXP-001; no final-current file mapping exists.
- **Options:** Name current Drive file IDs and effective dates, or confirm that no source is publishable yet.

## 3. Approved tone rules versus example copy

- **Question:** Which exact lines in the messaging guide are durable rules or approved canonical copy, and which are working examples?
- **Why it matters:** The guide mixes explicit wording instructions with expanded copy that conflicts with current management rules.
- **Affects:** All copy generation and review.
- **Current evidence:** BRD-003, GOV-003, MGMT-001.
- **Options:** Approve a rule-only extract; approve selected exact canonical lines; mark all long-form examples as working/historical.

## 4. Discovery terminology

- **Question:** Is the restriction limited to the Greek noun «ανακάλυψη», or does it also restrict `discover / ανακαλύπτω`?
- **Why it matters:** Current sources disagree.
- **Affects:** Cellar language, search ads, social and website copy.
- **Current evidence:** BRD-003 prohibits «Ανακάλυψη» while BRD-001/002 use discovery language positively.
- **Options:** Prohibit only the noun; prohibit the whole word family; or define channel/context-specific use.

## 5. Canonical visual production package

- **Question:** Which palette file supplies the canonical technical colour values, and which font files/fallbacks are licensed for print, web and social?
- **Why it matters:** The final boards establish direction but not a safe production token/package mapping.
- **Affects:** Design, mockups, website and templated content.
- **Current evidence:** VIS-001 and VIS-008/009.
- **Options:** Name the master palette and licensed package; provide approved replacements/fallbacks; or restrict work to supplied final templates.

## 6. Approved photography and usage rights

- **Question:** Which Drive photographs/illustrations are approved, and what usage, talent, territory and expiry restrictions apply?
- **Why it matters:** Approval and legal usage cannot be inferred from folder placement.
- **Affects:** Social, PPC, website, PR and design.
- **Current evidence:** ASSET-001/002; the source guide points to approved 2026 photography but the exact set is not connector-visible.
- **Options:** Approve a file-ID manifest with rights; or nominate a rights-cleared subset.

## 7. Current objective measurement

- **Question:** What are the effective 90-day period, owners, baselines, source systems and reporting cadence for Restaurant decline, Piazza covers/capacity and Cellar rolling sales?
- **Why it matters:** The targets are management-sourced but not yet analysis-ready.
- **Affects:** Social/PPC strategy, optimisation, reporting and evaluation.
- **Current evidence:** MGMT-001 and PPC-002–005.
- **Options:** Confirm the current figures and definitions; revise them; or retain them as directional objectives without automated evaluation.

## 8. Google Ads export boundary

- **Question:** Which account/customer or campaign identifiers reliably isolate Monzù data from the unrelated Homebrew rows?
- **Why it matters:** Analysis without a verified filter would be misleading.
- **Affects:** PPC reporting, optimisation and future evals.
- **Current evidence:** PPC-003/004/005.
- **Recommendation:** Supply the Monzù account/customer ID or approve an explicit campaign inclusion list.
