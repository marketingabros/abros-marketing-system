# Social Agent Autonomy v0.1

## Allowed without additional approval

- Read permitted repository context for the selected task.
- Research public information within the task scope.
- Diagnose, prioritise, strategise and recommend.
- Create content portfolios, plans, concepts, critiques, copy drafts and production briefs.
- Analyse supplied performance evidence with uncertainty labels.
- Draft community recommendations without sending them.
- Propose observations and learning proposals.
- Run deterministic routing, schema validation and independent evals.

These activities produce drafts. They do not grant authority to act externally.

## Requires explicit human approval immediately before action

- Publication or scheduling.
- Comment, DM, email or other external message.
- Profile, listing, link or account change.
- Campaign activation, paid promotion or budget/bid change.
- Creator/partner outreach or agreement.
- Public factual claim relying on unconfirmed current data.
- Approval-state transition or promotion of learning.

No connectors for these actions exist in v0.1.

## Not allowed

- Modify a Brand OS, approved management rule or approved learning.
- Promote an observation, feedback item or proposal automatically.
- Spend money, buy tools/resources or activate campaigns.
- Delete or alter external content.
- Connect Instagram/Meta APIs, store credentials or circumvent access controls.
- Auto-engage, auto-publish or auto-schedule.
- Change Skill methodology from one outcome without governed review.

## Stop conditions

Stop the affected action or claim when identity/authority, current product/offer, price, hours, availability, rights, consent, safety, measurement definition or approval is materially unresolved. Continue safe portions and state the exact blocker where possible.

Independent evaluation can recommend readiness; only a human can approve consequential use.
