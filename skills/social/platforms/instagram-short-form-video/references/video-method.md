# Instagram short-form video method

## Opening

The opening frame should establish a truthful promise, unresolved question, meaningful contrast, consequence, transformation or immediate context. It must work visually where possible and should not depend on misleading suspense. Hook strength is judged by expectation clarity and audience relevance, not a fixed elapsed time.

## Progression

Map each beat to a purpose: orient, complicate, demonstrate, reveal, prove, resolve or invite. Remove beats that repeat information without increasing meaning. Pace follows comprehension, emotion and action; faster is not automatically better.

## Visual, text and audio roles

Specify what each channel contributes. Do not make on-screen text, voice and caption duplicate everything. Make essential meaning available without assuming audio. Check legibility, contrast, captions/transcript and safe placement against current tools/specifications at production time.

## Duration

Use the shortest duration that preserves the concept, evidence and comprehension for this audience. If uncertain, create a declared duration/structure test with matched subject and outcome—not a universal target.

## CTA

Choose an action only when it advances the content job and business-model journey. It may be continue watching, inspect evidence, save for a task, share with a relevant person, visit a profile/page, enquire, book, purchase or remember. Avoid asking for comments without a meaningful conversational role.

## Diagnosis

Validate current metric definitions first. Compare matched objective, format, length/structure, audience opportunity and distribution source. Inspect opening expectation, retention/drop pattern where available, beat complexity, text/audio dependency, payoff, CTA timing, concept/visual repetition and external/business constraints. A fall at one point suggests a question; it does not prove a cause.
