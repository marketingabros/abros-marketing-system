# Paid Creative Resource Shortlist

Phase: 3.6 — cross-industry social creative strategy and critique  
Research date: 2026-09-07  
Status: market search and procurement decision only; no purchase, ingestion, Skill construction or agent construction is authorised.

## Management decision

**BUY NONE**

The market contains strong creative-strategy education, but this search did not find a resource that clears all three requirements for Social v0.1:

1. a deep, repeatable creative strategy and critique method;
2. convincing social-native application across Abros business models; and
3. written rights allowing the intended internal AI-assisted synthesis or encoding.

The strongest content candidates fail the licensing gate. BetterBriefs expressly prohibits putting its materials into AI systems or using its methodology to build or improve software/tools. LIONS Learning similarly prohibits AI/LLM upload and derivative use. Other close candidates do not publish sufficiently clear internal adaptation and AI-use rights. Under the approved 100-point rubric, a high content score cannot compensate for this.

Abros should proceed with free/official expertise and its own documented operating experiments. Management may contact no more than the three conditional leads below for samples and written usage rights, but should not buy any of them under the public terms reviewed.

## Search scope and method

- **21 resources investigated** across courses, practitioner programmes, case libraries and professional training.
- **15 serious candidates scored** against `PAID_RESOURCE_EVALUATION_RUBRIC.yaml`.
- **6 candidates screened out** before full scoring because the offer was stale, duplicative, too broad, prompt/AI-led, paid-media/ecommerce-led, or designed for personal-authority publishing.
- Public provider pages, current course pages and published terms were reviewed. No paid material was accessed or reproduced.
- Prices are recorded in the provider's published currency. “Not public” means management would need a quote; no currency conversion was invented.
- The evaluation rewards evidence visible before purchase. Claims hidden behind checkout receive no benefit of the doubt.

## Conditional due-diligence shortlist — not purchase recommendations

These are the only resources worth a rights-and-sample enquiry. They are not approved for purchase.

### 1. BetterBriefs — Assessing Creative Ideas

**Public price:** US$595  
**Format:** three-week online course; seven modules, approximately five hours plus live Q&A  
**Update/currentness:** next course listed for 12 October 2026; terms updated 6 July 2026  
**Rubric score:** 85/100 before gates  
**Decision:** CONDITIONAL LEAD — fails the licensing gate and is not social-native enough to buy unseen

**What it appears to teach**

- A systematic method for distinguishing an idea from its execution.
- Rational and emotional bias in evaluation.
- Selecting ideas, preserving the core idea through development, and giving more useful feedback.
- Cross-category application through course exercises and discussion.

**Why it is the closest content fit**

It directly addresses the most important gap identified in Phase 3.5: repeatable idea assessment, selection and critique. The instructors have substantial agency strategy and effectiveness experience, and the current course description is more decision-oriented than generic social-media training.

**Material limitations**

- It evaluates advertising ideas rather than teaching a complete social-native territory-to-format system.
- Public evidence does not show enough failed cases, organic social work, fatigue diagnosis or analytics linkage.
- Its July 2026 terms prohibit uploading materials to AI systems and using the materials or methodology to train, prompt, build, benchmark or improve AI/software/tools. They also restrict derivative works.

**Portfolio transferability:** HIGH in principle across local venue/experience, local service, ecommerce/product and an unknown future industry because idea-evaluation questions are category-independent. Social-native examples and business-model constraints would still need to be supplied by Abros.

**What we would extract if expressly licensed:** general decision stages, evaluator questions, bias checks and accept/reject/revise logic, rewritten from first principles and tested by Abros.  
**What we must not copy:** course text, recordings, slides, templates, named proprietary frameworks, screenshots, examples or any close derivative. Under current terms, we must also not enter the materials or methodology into an AI system.  
**Material improvement over current research:** YES for human creative evaluation discipline; NOT YET for a licensable Social Core method.

**Evidence required before reconsideration**

1. A representative sample showing the diagnostic steps, rejected options, worked critique and failure analysis.
2. Examples of the method applied to organic social work—not only campaign advertising.
3. Written permission explicitly allowing Abros to create its own internal, non-commercial decision logic and evals informed by general learning, including precise boundaries on human notes, AI use and retained derivatives.
4. Confirmation that Abros is eligible, given the terms' “Restricted Provider” and comparable-tool provisions.

**Proof-of-value test**

Two human reviewers independently score four unseen concepts (venue, local service, ecommerce and unfamiliar future model) before and after training. Improvement must appear in rejection agreement, reasoning quality, distinctiveness and commercial fit. Purchased materials must not be entered into an AI system during the test unless written permission is obtained.

Sources: [course page](https://www.betterbriefs.com/assessing-creative-ideas-course), [academy and price](https://www.betterbriefs.com/academy), [instructor background](https://www.betterbriefs.com/about), [2026 terms](https://www.betterbriefs.com/terms_conditions).

### 2. Contagious — Creative Accelerator

**Public price:** €899 / £799 / US$999  
**Format:** six-week online programme; approximately eight hours, mainly video with tests and a final live session  
**Update/currentness:** public course page was active; it states that cases are drawn from the preceding 18 months, but no page-update date was shown  
**Rubric score:** 85/100 before gates  
**Decision:** CONDITIONAL LEAD — fails the licensing gate

**What it appears to teach**

- Creativity evaluation, cultural relevance and organising principles.
- “Heretical questions” and creative tactics intended to unlock stronger work.
- More than 75 recent campaigns across multiple markets, with interviews and case analysis.

**Why it is a strong content lead**

It has the best publicly evidenced combination of recent cross-industry cases, structured strategic devices and creative inspiration. It may improve territory formation and help Abros avoid category conventions.

**Material limitations**

- It is advertising-led rather than demonstrably social-native.
- Public evidence is stronger on successful cases than rejected routes, weak work and diagnostic correction.
- LIONS Learning grants limited viewing for professional purposes, prohibits derivative works, and expressly prohibits uploading content to AI/LLM systems. The published licence therefore does not permit the intended encoding use.

**Portfolio transferability:** HIGH across current and future business models at the territory and cultural-pattern level; MEDIUM for local conversion and ongoing organic-social mechanics, which are not its visible centre of gravity.

**What we would extract if expressly licensed:** abstract territory-development questions, cultural-relevance checks, critique stages and case-derived failure patterns, restated and independently validated by Abros.  
**What we must not copy:** videos, case write-ups, images, tests, interviews, proprietary labels/frameworks or derivative course material. Current terms also prohibit AI/LLM upload.  
**Material improvement over current research:** YES for cross-industry case breadth and creative territory stimulation; NOT YET for licensable social-native operating logic.

**Evidence required before reconsideration**

1. A module sample demonstrating repeatable inputs, branches, rejection criteria and failed-case analysis—not only inspirational cases.
2. Proof that the method works for ongoing organic social systems as well as campaigns.
3. A negotiated organisational licence explicitly permitting Abros-authored internal synthesis, evals and AI-assisted application without reproducing proprietary materials.
4. Defined team access, retention and post-course use.

**Proof-of-value test**

Apply the permitted method to the same four unseen briefs used for BetterBriefs. Blind reviewers must see a material increase in territory distinctiveness and business fit without convergence toward award-advertising conventions.

Sources: [course page](https://learning.lions.co/pages/contagious-creative-accelerator-updated), [LIONS Learning terms](https://learning.lions.co/pages/terms-and-conditions), [terms of use](https://learning.lions.co/pages/terms-of-use).

### 3. D&AD — Creative Evaluation

**Public price:** quote required; team-only delivery  
**Format:** three-hour masterclass  
**Update/currentness:** included in D&AD's 2026 masterclass catalogue; specific page-update date not shown  
**Rubric score:** 81/100 before gates  
**Decision:** CONDITIONAL LEAD — licensing and case depth unverified

**What it appears to teach**

- A shared language for assessing creative work.
- D&AD's Creative Compass as an evaluation device.
- Giving feedback and aligning teams around creative quality.

**Why it remains in due diligence**

It is tightly aligned to human critique and could improve evaluator consistency with limited time investment. Team delivery may also be more useful than an individual certificate.

**Material limitations**

- Public material does not demonstrate enough decision trees, failed cases or before/after critique.
- It is not explicitly social-native and has limited visible analytics/commercial linkage.
- Price, sample material, retention, adaptation and AI-assisted internal-use rights are not public.

**Portfolio transferability:** HIGH in principle across all four required contexts because a shared critique language should be category-independent; commercial and format-specific tests would remain Abros-owned.

**What we would extract if expressly licensed:** generic evaluation dimensions, review sequencing and human-feedback practices, expressed as an Abros-authored rubric after proof-of-value testing.  
**What we must not copy:** the Creative Compass, workshop materials, slides, examples, recordings or branded/proprietary language unless the organisational licence expressly allows it.  
**Material improvement over current research:** POSSIBLY, through reviewer consistency and critique language; evidence is insufficient to claim a material improvement now.

**Evidence required before reconsideration**

1. Full quote and organisational licence.
2. A sample showing how Creative Compass changes an actual accept/reject/revise decision.
3. Failed or contested cases and facilitator critique—not just award examples.
4. Written permission defining whether Abros may translate general learning into its own internal rubric and use that rubric with AI, without copying D&AD materials.

**Proof-of-value test**

Use a facilitated pilot with two independent reviewers and six mixed-quality social concepts. Measure inter-reviewer agreement, quality of revision instructions and detection of brand/commercial weakness against the existing Abros baseline.

Sources: [masterclass page](https://www.dandad.org/learning/masterclasses/creative-evaluation), [2026 masterclass catalogue](https://media.dandad.org/documents/Masterclass_catalogue_2026-New_Edit_2.pdf).

## Why apparently strong alternatives were not shortlisted

- **LIONS Creative MBA:** excellent expert and case access, but €2,849 is disproportionate before a bounded proof of need; it overlaps Creative Accelerator, is not demonstrably social-native, no future cohort was visible after the April 2026 intake, and the same LIONS rights restrictions apply.
- **BetterBriefs Brief Writing:** strong methodology but solves briefing more than concept development and critique. It also carries the same explicit AI/tool-building restrictions.
- **Sweathead / Strategy Finishing School:** credible strategy practitioners and useful structured thinking, but insufficient public failed-case evidence, limited social-native depth and unclear internal encoding rights.
- **Link in Bio:** probably the best current social-native case stream reviewed, but it is an editorial archive rather than a repeatable creative decision system. It could be reconsidered later as monitored external research—not as a Skill source.
- **IPA Advanced Building a Brand on Social:** promising social translation, but the one-day public syllabus does not evidence the methodological or case depth required to fix the 8.0/10 gap.

## Exact value needed to justify purchase

A purchase becomes rational only if a provider supplies all of the following:

- a repeatable path from business/brand tension to creative territory, concept, format and execution;
- explicit accept/reject/revise criteria with trade-offs and stop conditions;
- recent organic-social examples across at least two materially different business models;
- rejected routes, failed work, before/after critique and corrected decisions;
- a method that complements—not duplicates—official platform guidance and existing Abros research;
- written organisational rights permitting Abros-authored internal synthesis, durable notes/evals and explicitly bounded AI-assisted use;
- a pilot that improves blinded decision quality against Abros's existing baseline.

Without those conditions, management is buying professional development or inspiration—not portable expertise for the operating system.

## Complementarity decision

No pair is recommended. BetterBriefs Assessing Creative Ideas plus Contagious Creative Accelerator would be the most complementary content pair—evaluation discipline plus territory/case breadth—but both currently fail the same licensing requirement and neither sufficiently closes the social-native operating gap. Buying both would amplify overlap and legal ambiguity, not solve it.

## Recommendation for Social v0.1

Proceed without paid material. Build Abros-authored v0.1 methods from public/official research and explicitly recorded internal experiments. Use the Phase 3.5 four-brief blind test as the baseline. If management chooses to conduct vendor due diligence, ask the three conditional leads for samples and rights in the order shown; rescore only new written evidence.

The missing expertise most likely to lift the current **8.0/10 creative/commercial usefulness score** is still senior, social-native **concept selection and critique**: explaining why one territory should be chosen, another rejected, how the concept changes by format and business model, and when performance evidence warrants revision or retirement. This search confirms the gap, but it does not justify purchasing a currently available resource under the evidence and rights reviewed.
