# Community triage and social-proof method

## Triage

Classify the item before drafting:

- `ROUTINE`: factual, low-risk and answerable from approved truth;
- `SENSITIVE`: complaint, personal information, emotionally charged issue or uncertain fact;
- `CONSEQUENTIAL`: safety, health, allergy, child/privacy issue, threat, legal claim, compensation, discrimination, harassment, creator/payment dispute or material operational promise;
- `DO_NOT_ENGAGE`: spam, manipulation, credible bad-faith escalation risk or content requiring platform/reporting procedures.

Then select an objective: answer, acknowledge, clarify, move securely to an owner, request permission, thank, learn, or decline engagement. A response is not always the right action.

## Proof selection

Assess proof by relevance to the customer's uncertainty, authenticity, specificity, recency, representativeness, rights/consent and operational truth. Distinguish customer evidence, expert evidence, process evidence, demonstration, aggregate signal and brand claim. One testimonial does not establish a universal outcome.

For UGC record creator/source, original URL/file, permission scope, territories/channels, duration, edits allowed, attribution, compensation/gift/affiliate status, people/minor consent and expiry/revocation. Platform disclosure and music/media rights remain separate checks.

## Drafting boundary

Use only facts supplied by Brand OS or verified runtime operations. Match tone from the Brand OS, but never copy one brand's response into another. Avoid defensive certainty. For sensitive cases, acknowledge without litigating facts publicly, preserve evidence and route to the named owner.

Every external response—including routine drafts—requires human approval in v0.1. Consequential cases remain `ESCALATED_NO_REPLY` until the responsible human decides.
