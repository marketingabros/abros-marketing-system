# Social strategy method

## 1. Resolve the real objective

Rewrite the business objective as a dated state change with an owner and constraint. Separate demand creation, demand capture, retention and reputation. If management has supplied several objectives, expose the trade-off and select a primary objective rather than pretending all are equal.

## 2. Select the customer behaviour

Choose the next meaningful change social can plausibly influence:

- awareness: recognise or recall a relevant proposition;
- consideration: reduce an uncertainty or increase preference;
- visit: attend a location or experience;
- booking: commit to a dated/capacity-bound experience;
- purchase: complete a product transaction;
- repeat behaviour: return, reorder or adopt a habit;
- community: contribute, advocate or sustain a relationship.

Define the behaviour concretely. “Engage” is not enough.

## 3. Define social's role

Choose one primary role: create discovery, make the proposition understandable, supply proof, reduce friction, create timely action, sustain memory, support repeat behaviour, or facilitate community. Social may contribute to an outcome without causing it alone.

## 4. Design content jobs

A content job explains what uncertainty, motivation or action the content addresses. Useful jobs include reveal, demonstrate, compare, orient, reassure, prove, invite, remind, answer and reconnect. Do not turn these into universal pillars; select only jobs needed for the objective and journey.

## 5. Specify measurement before content

For each job define:

- activity/output: what was produced/distributed;
- audience response: observable attention, understanding or action signal;
- outcome: business-model conversion or relationship change;
- impact: the approved business result, with attribution limits.

Record metric definition, source, denominator, window and known confounders. A platform metric is evidence for a job, not automatically the objective.

## 6. Check feasibility and conflict

Test against capacity, availability, geography, fulfilment, price/offer truth, production resources and human approval. If demand is already constrained, the correct strategy may shift periods, protect experience quality or build future memory rather than maximise reach.

## 7. State confidence

Label each important recommendation `DATA_SUPPORTED`, `STRONG_HEURISTIC`, `EXPERIMENTAL` or `INSUFFICIENT_DATA`. Name what evidence would change the decision.
