# Creative territory, concept and critique method

## Definitions

- **Territory:** a repeatable strategic space created from a customer tension, brand truth and commercial job.
- **Concept:** one organising idea within a territory that determines what the audience experiences and why it matters.
- **Format:** the platform container or interaction pattern.
- **Execution:** the specific narrative, copy, visual, audio and action choices.
- **Asset:** the produced file or component.

Never accept a format, topic or production activity as a concept without the organising idea.

## Development

1. State the behaviour to influence and the obstacle/tension.
2. Identify a relevant, provable brand truth from the active Brand OS.
3. Generate at least three territories using different mechanisms—not word variations. Possible mechanisms include reveal, contrast, transformation, participation, demonstration, anticipation, serial discovery, social proof, ritual, reframing or useful constraint.
4. For each territory, write the audience reason to care, brand right-to-play, commercial role, social-native participation/distribution mechanism and likely failure.
5. Develop concepts only from territories that survive the first review.
6. Choose format because it strengthens the idea: motion, sequence, persistence, interaction, immediacy or comparison.
7. Check recent brand history for repeated promise, subject, opening, visual device, format and CTA.

Pattern interruption means breaking a category or feed expectation in service of meaning. Random noise, forced slang, spectacle without relevance and trend imitation do not qualify.

## Evaluation framework

Judge every criterion with `STRONG`, `REVISE`, `FAIL` or `UNKNOWN`, and give a reason:

1. **Behaviour:** What business/customer behaviour should change?
2. **Core idea:** Can the organising thought be stated without describing the format?
3. **Care:** Why would this audience give attention or act?
4. **Social-native:** Does participation, sequence, immediacy, sharing, conversation or feed context strengthen it?
5. **Brand right:** Which supplied brand truth makes this credible and ownable?
6. **Distinctiveness:** Could another brand run it unchanged? If yes, what must become specific?
7. **Novelty:** Is it meaningfully new relative to recent brand history, or intentionally repeated with a reason?
8. **Tension/interest:** Is there a question, contrast, consequence, reveal, useful friction or emotional stake?
9. **Commercial role:** Does it advance the selected behaviour without forcing a sale into every execution?
10. **Execution:** Can it be produced truthfully within assets, rights, capacity and time?
11. **Restraint:** Is it trying too hard, overexplaining or layering devices that dilute the idea?
12. **Communication:** Is it communicating, proving or enabling something rather than decorating?
13. **Format fit:** Does the chosen format materially strengthen the concept?
14. **Subtraction:** What can be removed while preserving or strengthening the idea?
15. **Risk/learning:** What might fail, how would we detect it, and what would we revise?

## Decision rules

- `SELECT`: behaviour, core idea, brand right, commercial role and feasibility are strong; no unresolved fatal issue.
- `REVISE`: the central mechanism is valuable but one or more execution/clarity/distinctiveness issues can be repaired.
- `REJECT`: no meaningful audience value, no brand right, decoration only, copied mechanism/execution, incompatible commercial purpose, material rights/truth risk, or repair would require a different idea.
- `INSUFFICIENT_DATA`: brand fit, operational truth, history or audience need cannot be judged.

Do not total arbitrary numeric scores. A fatal weakness cannot be hidden by several cosmetic strengths.

## Required adversarial response pattern

Input: “Show behind the scenes and ask followers to comment.”

Diagnosis: this names access and an engagement action, not a concept. It lacks a behaviour target, tension, specific reason to care, brand right, progression and meaningful comment role. “Behind the scenes” may also be indistinguishable from competitors.

Improvement method: find a consequential process, decision, transformation, constraint or unresolved question that the audience could not normally see. Turn it into a sequence with stakes and proof. Invite a response only when the audience's choice, experience or knowledge changes the next episode or resolves a genuine question. The actual improved concept must use the loaded Brand OS and business-model truth; do not invent one generically.
