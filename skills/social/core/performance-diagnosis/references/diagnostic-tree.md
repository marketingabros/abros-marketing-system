# Performance diagnostic tree

Follow this order because an invalid metric can make every later inference false.

## 1. Define the symptom

Record exact measure, numerator, denominator, uniqueness rule, source, content/surface scope, period, timezone and extraction date. State whether the change is absolute, rate-based or mix-driven.

## 2. Test comparability

Compare like objective, format, audience opportunity, period length, lifecycle stage and distribution conditions where possible. Split follower/non-follower, organic/paid and content types when available. Use the brand's matched baseline before an external benchmark. If the sample is small or volatile, describe range and uncertainty rather than inventing significance.

## 3. Check gates and instrumentation

- metric rename, missing records, duplicated views or denominator change;
- current account/recommendation eligibility and platform status;
- tracking/link/booking/checkout changes;
- paid distribution, collaboration or unusual audience source.

Retrieve material Instagram facts from `research/INSTAGRAM_CURRENT_KNOWLEDGE.md`; do not rely on memory.

## 4. Generate competing hypotheses

Cover only relevant branches, but consider:

1. distribution opportunity or follower/non-follower mix;
2. format or topic mix;
3. opening promise, retention, pacing or expectation mismatch;
4. concept repetition, visual sameness, offer repetition or fatigue;
5. engagement quality—shares/saves/comments/profile actions in context;
6. journey friction or weak/irrelevant CTA;
7. audience/catchment mismatch;
8. cadence or campaign-stage change;
9. seasonality, events or demand timing;
10. capacity, availability, stock, fulfilment or service constraints;
11. insufficient sample or random variation.

For each hypothesis record supporting, contradicting and missing evidence. Rank by evidence and decision value, not narrative appeal.

## 5. Choose a proportionate test

Prefer one main variable, a matched comparison, an objective-linked measure, a defined observation window and a predeclared `continue`, `revise`, `stop` or `escalate` rule. Preserve current truths and human approval. A platform-native trial is not automatically a controlled experiment.

## 6. Separate conclusion levels

- `DATA_SUPPORTED`: directly supported by defined, relevant evidence.
- `STRONG_HEURISTIC`: credible method/pattern but not established in this case.
- `EXPERIMENTAL`: reasonable action specifically framed as a test.
- `INSUFFICIENT_DATA`: cause or choice cannot yet be supported.
