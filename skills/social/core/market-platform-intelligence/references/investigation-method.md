# Market and platform investigation method

## Scope first

Define the decision this research should inform, geography, time window, platform surface, business model and comparator logic. A competitor is not automatically a useful comparator; include alternatives competing for the same behaviour, occasion, need or attention.

## Evidence classes

- `PLATFORM_FACT`: current primary platform documentation or verified account-level state; include source, check date, scope, confidence and refresh condition.
- `PRACTITIONER_HEURISTIC`: attributed expert operating judgment that remains testable.
- `OBSERVATION`: directly visible behaviour in the defined sample; no outcome claim.
- `HYPOTHESIS`: an explanation or opportunity requiring evidence/test.

For Instagram, retrieve platform claims from `research/INSTAGRAM_CURRENT_KNOWLEDGE.md`. Secondary newsletters can trigger a check but cannot confirm a fact.

## Convention and whitespace analysis

Code the sample by customer job, proposition, proof type, territory, opening device, narrative structure, format, CTA, audience/occasion and repetition. Identify:

- crowded conventions with low meaningful difference;
- strategically necessary conventions that improve comprehension;
- unanswered customer uncertainties;
- underused brand truths or proof mechanisms;
- execution patterns to avoid copying;
- opportunities whose value depends on a missing assumption.

Whitespace is not “something nobody posted.” It is a relevant strategic space a brand has the right and ability to occupy.

## Trend handling

Record origin/observed spread, mechanism, current shelf-life evidence, platform fit, brand fit, rights/safety, saturation and adaptation distance. Preserve the mechanism only if it serves the objective; do not imitate the execution. Mark the result experimental.

## Platform-change handling

Detect -> locate primary source/in-app evidence -> record old/new state -> determine affected surfaces/accounts/regions -> assess decision impact -> retain/revise/retire/test through human review. Never silently edit a durable Skill.
